# 🤖 YU Combat Robotics

> 🚧 **In the works.** This is a proposed student club at the Lassonde School of Engineering and isn't official yet. Currently gauging interest and laying the groundwork, so if this sounds like your thing, feel free to reach out!

The idea: a student run club at **York University** that designs, builds, and battles combat robots.

## What we're hoping to do

- **Design & build** combat robots from scratch, from CAD and machining to electronics and drive systems
- **Compete** in combat robotics events against other schools and builders
- **Teach** hands-on engineering skills: mechanical design, fabrication, wiring, motor control, and code
- **Test, break, repeat** every fight lets us itterate and improve for the next build

## Where we'd start

The plan is to start small and beginner friendly:

- 🪶 **Antweight (1 lb)** — cheap, fast to build, great for learning
- 🐞 **Beetleweight (3 lb)** — a possible next step once we've got the basics down
- 👀 **Larger weight classes** — down the road, if the club grows and we have the space and resources

## Who could join

**Anyone.** No experience or engineering major needed. We'd need builders, designers, programmers, and people interested in helping with events, social media, and sponsorships.
